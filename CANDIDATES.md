## Winner: #2

> I didn't miss the deadline — I gave it a head start and it never looked back.

**Rationale.** One sentence, one excuse, one turn: the miss becomes an act of
sportsmanship ("gave it a head start") and the deadline itself runs away
("never looked back"), so the speaker is the victim of their own generosity.
It sounds like something a cornered person would actually say, exaggerated
just enough, and the punch lands on the last three words. 77 characters,
79 bytes, 17 words, single sentence.
